# Lifesaver-ai

Open-source AI for safety, protection and human empowerment, in English and Portuguese.

Inteligência artificial open source para segurança, proteção e capacitação das pessoas, em inglês e português.

## Modules / Módulos

- **Document Guardian**: helps you understand documents and spot risky clauses.
- **Scam Detector**: helps you recognise scams, fake messages and fraud.
- **Money Protector**: helps you protect your money and avoid financial traps.
- **Global Helper**: helps with everyday safety questions anywhere in the world.

Each module is a skill in the `skills/` folder.

## Important notice / Aviso importante

Lifesaver-ai does not replace a lawyer or a financial advisor. For serious legal or financial decisions, talk to a qualified professional.

O Lifesaver-ai não substitui um advogado nem um consultor financeiro. Para decisões legais ou financeiras importantes, fala com um profissional qualificado.

## How to use / Como usar

Open the `SKILL.md` file of the module you want, inside `skills/`, and follow the instructions. Detailed installation guides will be added to the `docs/` folder.
